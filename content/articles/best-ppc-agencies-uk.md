## What makes a PPC agency one of the best?

A great PPC agency isn't the one with the biggest client logos. It's the one that ties every click to your unit economics. The strongest agencies share four traits: transparent reporting you actually understand, a defined objective before any campaign goes live, continuous testing of creative and audiences, and cost management that protects your margin as you scale. Anything less and you're renting traffic, not building an acquisition engine.

Here's the distinction most buyers miss. Plenty of agencies optimise toward a *platform* metric: cost per click, click-through rate, or conversions counted inside Google Ads. Fewer optimise toward a *business* metric: profit per acquired customer after delivery costs. An agency that doesn't ask about your margins, average order value, or repeat-purchase rate can hit every dashboard target and still lose you money. The best PPC agencies start with your numbers, not the ad account.

## How do you choose the right PPC agency?

Start with your numbers, not their pitch. Before you sign with any PPC agency, you should be able to state your target cost per acquisition, your average order value, and the margin you need to protect, and the agency should ask for all three unprompted. Small differences in management quality compound quickly across a year of spend, because every percentage point of conversion rate is a percentage point off your cost per customer.

Work through this checklist when comparing agencies:

- **Do they define success before launch?** A defined objective and success measure should precede any spend.
- **Is reporting transparent and plain-English?** You should understand exactly where budget goes and what it returns.
- **Do they manage to margin, not just clicks?** Ask how they factor your delivery costs and AOV into bidding.
- **How do they test?** Look for systematic creative and audience testing, not set-and-forget campaigns.
- **What's the contract and exit?** Favour clear terms and account ownership that stays with you.

## Is a specialist PPC agency worth it?

For most businesses, yes, provided you choose well and your margins can carry it. Returns vary enormously by industry and by execution, which is precisely why the agency decision matters more than the platform decision. A specialist exists to move you up that range and keep you there as costs climb. Be wary of any agency quoting you an expected return before it has seen your margins.

And costs are climbing. Cost per click rose for 87% of industries, with some categories seeing increases above 40% ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks), 2025). That dataset is US-based, so treat it as direction rather than a UK benchmark, but the pressure is real on both sides of the Atlantic. In a market that competitive, expert execution is not a luxury. It is the difference between paid media that compounds and paid media that quietly drains the account.

## How much do PPC agencies charge in the UK?

PPC pricing has two parts: the agency's management fee and your ad spend. Most UK SMEs sit between £1.50 and £2.50 per click, with the national spread running roughly £0.40 to £4.00 depending on sector, and competitive fields like legal services averaging £8.25 ([Whito](https://whito.co.uk/research/uk-ppc-costs/), 2026). Agencies typically charge either a flat monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, or a performance-based fee. The right structure depends on your budget and how much strategic input you need.

The cheapest option is rarely the best value. A low management fee attached to poorly targeted spend costs far more than a fair fee attached to disciplined, margin-aware management. Judge agencies on return delivered rather than invoice size. That holds across every entry in this list.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best PPC agency in the UK?

There's no single best PPC agency. It depends on your size and goal. ### How much does a PPC agency cost in the UK?

Expect two costs: a management fee plus ad spend. Most UK SMEs pay between £1.50 and £2.50 per click, rising to around £3.65 in B2B SaaS and £8.25 in legal services ([Whito](https://whito.co.uk/research/uk-ppc-costs/), 2026). Management is usually a monthly retainer, a percentage of spend, or a performance fee. Total budgets vary widely by industry and competition.

### Is PPC better than SEO?

They solve different problems. PPC delivers visibility and traffic almost immediately, while [SEO](/seo) compounds slowly into lower-cost, durable rankings. Most growing businesses run both, PPC for speed and testing, SEO for long-term cost efficiency. The best agencies sequence the two around your economics rather than selling one in isolation.

### What does a PPC agency actually do?

A PPC agency plans, builds, and manages paid search and social campaigns, keyword and audience research, ad creative and copy, bidding, landing-page guidance, and reporting. Strong agencies tie every campaign to a defined objective and success measure, then continuously test and optimise to improve return on ad spend over time.

### How quickly does PPC deliver results?

Faster than SEO, often within days of launch, since ads appear at the top of search results immediately. Meaningful optimisation, though, takes longer: agencies need a few weeks of conversion data to refine bidding and targeting. Expect early traffic quickly and compounding efficiency over the first two to three months.
